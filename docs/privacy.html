LiveWall Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

What we collect

  • Anonymous ID. A random ID and secret created on first launch. No name, email or phone number is collected.
  • Push token. Used only to deliver your partner's notes to your phone.
  • Pairing data. The ID of the one partner you pair with.
  • Notes. The text (up to 100 characters), sender and time of notes you and your partner send.
  • Reports. If you block and report a partner, their last 10 notes and your reason are stored for review.

Videos and wallpapers you make stay on your device. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

How it is used and shared

Data is used only to run partner notes. It is stored with Cloudflare and delivered through Apple's push service. We do not sell or share it with anyone else.

Retention and deletion

Unpairing deletes all notes between you and your partner. Use Partner → ⋯ → Delete my data in the app to delete your account and all associated data. Purchases are handled by Apple.

Children

LiveWall is not directed at children under 13.
